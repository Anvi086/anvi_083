About me

Hello, I'm Anvi.

I'm from India and I now live in Australia. Growing up in one country and building a life in another taught me to listen first, adapt quickly and explain things clearly to people who see the world differently from me.

I aim to turn messy, unclear problems into simple, well-made things that people enjoy using. I like to understand the people behind a problem before I start solving it, then test my ideas early and often.

Right now I'm looking for a team that cares about its craft, where I can keep learning and contribute from my first week.
