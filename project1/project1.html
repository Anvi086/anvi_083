ghar.

An app that helps new Maharashtrian students settle into Melbourne.

Role
Researcher and designer
Tools
Figma, Miro
Time
add
Context
People Project, RMIT
Problem

Moving far from home is hard to organise.

New Maharashtrian students at RMIT have to sort out housing, groceries, money and friends in a new city, usually all in the first few weeks. I wanted to understand what makes that stretch easier or harder, then design something that helps.

Research

Five interviews, two personas.

I interviewed five students and ran a thematic analysis on the transcripts: labelling responses, comparing differences, mapping behavioural variables and looking for patterns. The patterns split into two groups, so I made two personas instead of one.

Solution

Five features for the first few weeks.

The app is called ghar., which means home in Marathi. I chose reds, oranges and yellows for the interface because they remind me of spices and feel warm.

Reflection

What I would do next.

Feedback on my first presentation asked for a conceptual model, validation with an interview participant, a clearer link between personas and problem statements, and a deeper thematic analysis. Those are the next steps I took.
