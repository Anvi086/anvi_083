Photo Booth Studio

A drag and drop photo strip maker for an events company.

Role
Designer and developer
Tools
HTML, CSS, JavaScript
Time
add
Context
Interactive Media, Assignment 3
Concept

A strip you build with your hands.

Photo Booth Studio is part of a bigger site for an events company that does weddings, birthdays and graduations. Guests make their own photo strip: drag a colour onto the frame, add three pictures, drop on stickers, then download the result. The visual theme is dark red with cream text.

Click a swatch below to see the frame idea in miniature.

Features

What it does.

Frame colours

Drag a colour onto the strip and the whole frame changes.

Three photos

Upload pictures from your laptop into the centred strip.

Stickers

Drag stickers onto the strip and resize them.

Download
