Case study 02 / 04UX redesign

MakeMyTrip

Fixing a flight search form that asks for too much at once.

Role
UX designer
Tools
Figma, Adobe Express
Time
add
Context
Principles Project, RMIT
Problem

An overwhelming booking flow.

The MakeMyTrip flight search puts trip type, fare type, a GST field, price drop protection and promotions in one crowded form. In my first 5-second test, all 5 participants knew it was a flight booking page and 4 of 5 remembered details like Mumbai to Melbourne, the dates and cheapest or fastest. The problem showed up once they had to actually do something.

Process

Test, redesign, test again.

I ran think-aloud tests on the original site, built a clickable redesign in Figma, then ran the same tasks on it to compare. I also ran a first click test, asking where people would click to compare flights.

Results

Faster on the redesign.

6:17average time on task, original site
3:15average time on task, redesign
23s to 12saverage first click time
10/15redesign participants who clicked the right place
Original6:17
Redesign3:15

Original times were 7:19, 5:21 and 6:12. Redesign times were about 4:00, 3:45 and 2:00. The first click test used 5 participants on the original and 15 on the redesign, so treat the comparison as a direction rather than proof.
